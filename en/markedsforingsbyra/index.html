Four kinds of agency

Advertising agency, digital agency or performance agency?

The words get used interchangeably, but the agencies deliver different things and charge in different ways. That is the most important thing to know before you choose.

Type of agencyWhat they deliverHow they chargeGood fit for
Advertising agencyCampaign ideas, brand, film, print and often TVUsually per project or by the hourBusinesses that need to build or change their brand and can wait for the effect
Digital agencyWebsites, online stores, apps and often SEOUsually per project, plus a fixed agreement for maintenanceBusinesses that need a new website or something technical built
Performance agencyPaid ads, tracking and ongoing optimisation towards sales or inquiriesUsually a fixed monthly fee or a percentage of ad spendBusinesses that want to know what every krone in ads brings back
ArcadsPaid ads on Google, Meta and Snapchat, plus the ads, videos and landing pages, made in-houseA share of what we earn you. No lock-inEstablished businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000

The middle column matters most. If the agency takes a percentage of ad spend, it earns more when you spend more, whatever the money brings in. With a fixed fee, the agency earns the same in a good month and a bad one. We wanted a model where we only earn when you do. How the models play out is explained in performance-based vs. fixed fee.

An advertising agency is the right choice if the goal is for people to remember your name in two years. If the phone needs to ring next month, you need someone working in the ad accounts every week.

Our pricing: We take a share of what we earn you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough for our share to come to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. How we calculate it.

Who we are not a good fit for.

Our model only works when the ads can create enough. So we may as well say it plainly:

  • An ad budget under NOK 30,000 a month. Our share then becomes too small to cover 10+ new ads a week and weekly follow-up.
  • You want ongoing SEO. We do not write content to rank organically or do technical SEO for clients.
  • You want someone to post on social media. We make ads, not organic posts.
  • You want a TV commercial or a film with a crew. Our videos are made for ad accounts: many variations, short, with the hook in the first second. See what we make.
  • You want to advertise on TikTok. We run Meta and Snapchat, not TikTok ads.
  • The goal is awareness, not sales. An advertising agency is a better fit.
  • The business is brand new. We need sales or inquiries to measure against, so we work with established businesses.

5 questions to ask a marketing agency.

Ask every agency you talk to, including us. Our answer is under each question.

  1. What do I pay you in a month when the ads do not work?
    Nothing. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. The ad budget goes directly to Google and Meta.
  2. Who makes the ads, and how many new ones do I get?
    Jona and Victor, in-house. 10+ new ads a week.
  3. What do you do before the first campaign?
    Check and set up tracking in Google Ads, Meta and GA4, so we know the numbers are right.
  4. What is in the report?
    Sales or inquiries, what they cost and what we changed. Every Monday.
  5. When do you say no to a client?
    When the ad budget is under NOK 30,000 a month, or when the business needs something we do not do, such as SEO or TV.

What people ask a marketing agency.

What does a marketing agency do?+
It plans, makes and runs the marketing for a business. Some agencies do everything from logos to websites. We do paid ads on Google, Meta and Snapchat, make the ads and landing pages ourselves, and set up the tracking that shows what the ads brought in as sales and inquiries.
What is the difference between an advertising agency and a digital agency?+
An advertising agency creates campaign ideas and brand work, often for film, print and TV. A digital agency builds websites, online stores and apps, and often does SEO. A performance agency runs paid ads and is measured on sales or inquiries. We are the last kind, with our own creative production on top.
What does a marketing agency cost?+
We take a share of what we earn you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough for our share to come to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.
Do you do SEO or social media posting?+
No. We do not run SEO for clients, and we do not post organically on social media. We also do not run TikTok ads or TV advertising. What we do is paid ads on Google, Meta and Snapchat, and everything it takes for them to sell.
Is there a lock-in period?+
No. You can cancel whenever you like. The work happens in your own ad accounts.
Do you work with businesses all over Norway?+
Yes. Our office is in Skien, and we hold meetings over video. Utvendig Renhold advertises in nine regions from Agder to Trondheim, and several of our clients are in Oslo. See marketing agency in Oslo.

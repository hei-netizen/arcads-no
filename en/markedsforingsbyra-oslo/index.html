Marketing agency Oslo

A marketing agency for Oslo, where the click costs the most.

For plumbers, electricians and roofers, the top-of-page bid on Google is more than three times as high in Oslo as in Bergen. That changes what your marketing has to do. Three things to take from the table:

  • Trades carry the biggest Oslo premium. The high bid for plumbers, electricians and roofers is more than three times as high in Oslo as in Bergen.
  • Not everything is more expensive in Oslo. Hairdressers cost NOK 4–12. Estate agents have a higher top bid in Bergen (NOK 322) than in Oslo (NOK 282), and locksmiths have the same top bid in Oslo and Bergen (NOK 305), but NOK 5–25 in Trondheim.
  • Accountants are the most expensive. Up to NOK 680 per click in Oslo. At a 10% conversion rate, that is NOK 6,800 per inquiry.

A bid is not the same as a price. Keyword Planner shows what advertisers bid to reach the top, not what you pay. In our own Oslo accounts, a search click cost NOK 52 for the moving company (Keyword Planner: NOK 35–119) and NOK 57 for move-out cleaning (NOK 35–100) in June–Sept 2026. What we paid landed inside the range.

What sets Oslo apart is the share of people who get in touch: 8.7% of clicks became an inquiry, against 13.4% in Trondheim. That is why each branch now has its own local landing page. 18 pages went live in the ads on 28 August 2026, and 102 ads now send people to a local page instead of a shared one.

The maths: why the landing page has to do more in Oslo.

Cost per lead is cost per click divided by conversion rate. You only partly control the cost per click, because competitors set it in the auction. The conversion rate is yours.

The Oslo moving company we work for paid NOK 52 per search click. At an 11.1% conversion rate, that came to NOK 467 per lead (June–Sept 2026). The same click price gives:

Conversion rateCost per lead at NOK 52 per click

For a plumber, the gap between cities gets large. At the Oslo top bid (NOK 252) and a 10% conversion rate, an inquiry costs NOK 2,520. At the Bergen top bid (NOK 69) with the same page, it costs NOK 690. An Oslo business that wants a Bergen price per lead needs a page that converts many times better, or customers who are worth more.

Mobile is where the rate disappears. At the move-out cleaning company, 20.2% of clicks converted on desktop and 10.7% on mobile. At the moving company it was 11.0% and 7.1% (Google Ads, 1 July–30 Sept 2026). That is why we build Oslo pages for the phone first: form and phone number visible without scrolling, one offer, and a price or price level where possible.

When the click is expensive

Four ways to make every Oslo click worth more.

You cannot negotiate the Oslo bids down. What you can do is get more out of every click you have already paid for.

Landing page

One page per service

The ad and the page should be about the same search. For Generalfinans, a new landing page delivered a 16.1% conversion rate and NOK 412 per lead in its first 30 days (as of 13 Aug 2026). We build the pages ourselves.

Retargeting

Bring back the ones who did not get in touch

The people who clicked without getting in touch are already paid for. At the Oslo move-out cleaning company, retargeting delivered leads at NOK 116, against NOK 211 on search. The moves in the Google accounts are on Google Ads agency Oslo.

Meta

Reach people before they search

On Facebook and Instagram you do not bid on the keyword, so you are not in the same auction as all the other plumbers. We do not have Oslo numbers for Meta yet. What we do have: QualiPet got 53 sales from NOK 3,148 in Meta ads (1–14 Sept 2026). About Meta Ads.

The ads

10+ new ads a week

Statics, UGC, motion and video, made in-house. With expensive clicks, every percentage point of click-through and conversion counts, and the ad decides who clicks. Creative production.

What Oslo businesses ask us.

What does marketing cost in Oslo?+
You set the ad budget yourself. What varies is the cost per click. In the accounts we run, a lead in Oslo cost NOK 202 for move-out cleaning, NOK 237 for facade and roof cleaning and NOK 413 for a moving company (June–Sept 2026).
Is it more expensive to advertise in Oslo than in the rest of Norway?+
For most trades, yes. The top bid for plumbers, electricians and roofers is more than three times as high in Oslo as in Bergen. Not everything is more expensive: hairdressers cost NOK 4–12 per click, and estate agents have a higher top bid in Bergen than in Oslo.

No. Someone searching for a plumber in Oslo needs a plumber now. That click is expensive, but it comes from the warmest customers. Meta, retargeting and a better landing page make every search click worth more. They do not replace them.

The math

What the budget buys in Bergen and in Oslo.

Cost per inquiry = cost per click divided by the share of clicks that get in touch. Pick a service, a budget and a conversion rate.

The cost per click is the midpoint of Google’s top-of-page bid estimate in each city (Keyword Planner, Oct 2026). If you buy more clicks than there are searches, the searches run out before the budget does.

Example: A plumber with NOK 30,000 a month and 10% conversion gets around 667 clicks and 67 inquiries in Bergen, at NOK 450 each. The same budget in Oslo gives around 187 clicks and 19 inquiries, at around NOK 1,600.

But the estimate is not the answer

At Utvendig Renhold, a click cost about NOK 22 in Bergen and about NOK 21 in Oslo (worked out from cost per lead and conversion rate). So the clicks were not cheaper in Bergen. The difference came from how many people sent a form: 12.3% in Bergen against 8.7% in Oslo. That is why a lead ended up at NOK 181 in Bergen and NOK 237 in Oslo.

The same account shows that Keyword Planner can be far off: Google estimated NOK 35–106 for “takvask oslo”, while we paid around NOK 21. Use the estimates to compare cities and industries, not to set your budget.

Real numbers from Bergen

Bergen in an account with nine regions.

Utvendig Renhold cleans roofs and facades for homeowners in nine regions. Each region has its own Google Ads campaign, with ads like “Fasadevask i Bergen”, its own budget and its own line in the Monday report.

Bergen delivered 81 leads in 90 days, just four fewer than Trondheim, the largest region. The price was NOK 181 per lead: fourth lowest of nine, NOK 7 below average and NOK 56 below Oslo. For a Bergen business, that means you do not need an Oslo budget to get volume.

In August we built a dedicated landing page for the Bergen branch, with local copy and the branch’s contact person. The ads moved there on 28 August, so the numbers on the right come almost entirely from the national pages. Since 9 September we have been testing local against national pages in a Google Ads experiment. Until it gives an answer, we do not claim the local page is better.

What Bergen businesses ask.

What does digital marketing cost in Bergen?+
You set the ad budget yourself. What varies is the cost per click and per inquiry. Google estimates NOK 21–69 for a top-of-page click on “rørlegger bergen” (plumber) and NOK 25–72 for “elektriker bergen” (electrician). In the account we run for Utvendig Renhold, a lead in Bergen cost NOK 181 (9 June – 6 Sept 2026).
Is it cheaper to advertise in Bergen than in Oslo?+
Often, but not always. Google’s upper top-of-page bid estimate is lower in Bergen than in Oslo for 14 of the 18 services we checked. At Utvendig Renhold the clicks still cost about the same (around NOK 22 in Bergen and NOK 21 in Oslo). Leads were cheaper in Bergen because more people sent a form: 12.3% against 8.7%.
You are based in Skien. Why not choose an agency in Bergen?+
Choose the agency that can show you numbers. The work happens in your own ad account, and we hold meetings over video. We have no office in Bergen. The campaign for Utvendig Renhold’s Bergen branch is run from Skien, and it delivered 81 leads in 90 days.
Are you an advertising agency?+
Not in the traditional sense. We make ads for Google, Meta and Snapchat and run them ourselves, and we build landing pages. We do not make TV commercials or print, and we do not do SEO or organic social media.
Can Bergen and the rest of Vestland share one campaign?+
They can, but we split them when the cost per lead differs. With a separate campaign for Bergen you see what an inquiry costs there, and each municipality should have one owner so the numbers per area are right.
What do you charge?+
You pay a share of what we earn you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. See the pricing page.

Data from Google Ads, Meta and other platforms

When a client grants Arcads access to their advertising accounts, we retrieve data through the official interfaces (Google Ads API and Meta Marketing API). This is mainly campaign and performance data such as impressions, clicks, costs and conversions, as well as ads and audience settings.

  • We use the data only to run, analyse and report on the client's own advertising.
  • We do not sell data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the client and the service providers listed below.
  • We do not use the data to build profiles of individuals or for our own advertising.
  • Leads a client receives through lead forms or landing pages we build are processed on the client's behalf. The client is then the controller and Arcads the processor.

Access can be revoked at any time from the ad account (Google Ads: Access and security; Meta: Business settings → Partners).

Cookies and tracking

arcads.no uses these tools:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google) — visitor statistics
  • Meta Pixel (Meta) — measuring and targeting our own ads on Facebook and Instagram
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft) — anonymised recordings of clicks and scrolling to improve the site

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalised ads in Meta's ad preferences and Google's ad settings.

Service providers that process data for us

We use these providers, under data processing agreements or equivalent terms:

  • Google (Workspace/email, Analytics, Ads)
  • Meta Platforms (Pixel, advertising tools)
  • Microsoft (Clarity)
  • Make (Celonis) — receiving and forwarding form submissions
  • Cal.com — meeting booking
  • GitHub (Microsoft) — website hosting
  • Notion — reports and project documents
  • Anthropic — AI tools used in analysis and production

Some providers store data outside the EU/EEA, mainly in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and leads that do not become clients: up to 24 months after last contact.
  • Client data: for the duration of the agreement and up to 12 months after.
  • Accounting records: 5 years, as required by the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act.
  • Data retrieved from clients' ad accounts: deleted or anonymised no later than 12 months after the engagement ends.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object to processing. Where you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email hei@arcads.no and we will reply within 30 days.

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

How to request data deletion

Email hei@arcads.no with the subject "Data deletion request" and include the email address or phone number the data is linked to. If you connected a Facebook, Instagram or Google account through us, you can also remove the access in that account's settings. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email, except for data we are legally required to keep.

Changes

We update this policy when we adopt new tools or change how we process data. The date at the top shows the latest change.

How we run Google Ads for moving.

These are the moves we use in the moving company account. We have written about the ring campaign for neighboring municipalities, retargeting and branded searches on the page about Google Ads in Oslo, with the numbers.

Measurement

Only bookings count

The conversion in the account is a submitted booking request, measured on the thank-you page after the form. Phone clicks and visits to the contact page do not count. That way Google learns from people who actually asked for help moving.

Cost per click

Do the math on conversion rate

A search click cost NOK 52 on average. With 11% conversion, that is NOK 467 per booking. If conversion drops to 6%, the same clicks cost over NOK 850 per booking. That is why the booking page is the first thing we look at.

Bidding

One change at a time

The search campaign runs on Maximize conversions with a target CPA, set from what a booking is worth to the client. We change one thing at a time and decide in advance when we will read the effect, so we know what worked.

Search terms

Competitor names rarely book

A search for a competitor’s name cost NOK 254 without a single booking before it was added as a negative keyword. Someone searching for another company has usually made up their mind.

What moving companies ask us.

What does a lead cost for a moving company on Google Ads?+
In the moving company account we run in Oslo, a booking request cost NOK 413 on average in June–Sept 2026, and NOK 467 on search. It is the only moving company account we have, so treat it as one data point, not an industry average.
Why is moving so expensive on Google?+
Many moving companies bid on the same searches, so clicks get expensive. In our account a search click cost NOK 52. That makes the conversion rate the most important number: 10% gives NOK 520 per booking, 5% gives over NOK 1,000.
Should we advertise outside Oslo?+
Yes, but in a separate campaign, so you see the cost on its own. For the moving company, we run the municipalities within 60 minutes as a separate ring campaign. In the first month, a booking there cost NOK 390. More about the ring campaign.
What about Performance Max?+
We use it for retargeting in the moving company account, where it delivered bookings at NOK 310. It also showed ads in mobile games, so we check the placement report. PMax now has a channel report, search terms and campaign-level negative keywords, so you can see where the money goes.
What kind of form converts best?+
A booking form with the moving date and addresses gives requests the moving company can price right away. In our account we only count submitted booking requests, not phone clicks.
What do you charge?+
You pay a share of the value we create, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.

What we make.

A selection of ads we've made for our clients:

Statics

Images with one clear message

Product image, price or offer, and one sentence that says why. Quick to make, so we can test many angles in a short time. For Mokki, simple statics have worked better than the client expected.

Video

Edited from real footage

Much of the best material is filmed by the client. Utvendig Renhold films on the job, and we edit it into ads with text, sound and the right format for each channel.

UGC

Creators, customers and ambassadors

Video filmed by ordinary people from our scripts, or interviews with real customers. We find the creators and write the scripts. How we work with UGC →

Motion graphics

Animation and voiceover

Text and product in motion, with voiceover where it fits. Useful when the client has no video, or when the message is easier to show than to say.

How we test the ads.

1

Angle before format

We start with why people buy. For Badstugutta, the angles that bring in leads have been that the sauna arrives fully assembled, a fixed price and delivery in around four weeks. Only once the angle is chosen do we decide on format.

2

Variations of what works

When an angle brings in sales or leads, we make new hooks, formats and lengths of it. New ads go into ad sets that already have data, so the test doesn't reset the learning phase.

3

Measured on sales and leads

An ad is judged on what it costs per sale or lead, not on impressions or clicks. What delivers gets more budget. The rest we switch off.

We use AI tools in production where they save time. Video in the ads is real footage, and the products are always real.

What people ask about production.

Which ad formats do you make?+
Statics, video, UGC, motion graphics, ads built on customer quotes and Snapchat formats, in the sizes the channels use (1:1, 4:5 and 9:16). Everything is made by us, and everything runs in ad accounts we follow ourselves.
How fast do you deliver?+
We make 10+ new ads every week for our clients. How quickly the first round is ready mostly depends on what images and video you already have. If you have little, we start with statics while video and UGC are in production.
Can we buy creative content without ad management?+
Usually not. We make content as part of running the ads, because the account is where we see what works, and because our fee is a share of what the ads create. Send a message to victor@arcads.no if you have a specific need, and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.
How do you use AI in production?+
Where it saves time: research, rough cuts, copy variations and adapting to different formats. Video in the finished ads is real footage, and the products are always real.
How do you know which ads work?+
We only know once they've run. Every new ad goes into a campaign that already has data, and is measured on sales or leads, not impressions. Mokki's CMO was surprised himself at how well simple statics performed: “Wow, even statics working this well! Great job, guys, fantastic.” That's why we never make only video.

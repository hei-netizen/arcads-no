What service businesses ask us.

What does a lead from Google Ads cost in Norway?+
It depends on the industry and the city. In the accounts we run, a lead cost NOK 163 for facade and roof cleaning (including branded searches), NOK 202 for move-out cleaning in Oslo, NOK 406 for business leasing and NOK 413 for a moving company in Oslo. The numbers are from June–Sept 2026 and count submitted forms and bookings. The full table, with caveats.
How big a budget does a service business need?+
Smart bidding with a target CPA needs around 15–30 conversions a month per campaign to work well. If a lead costs NOK 200, that is NOK 3,000–6,000 a month per campaign. If you have less, it is better to run one region well than five thinly.
Google Ads or Meta for leads?+
Google first for services people already search for, like plumbers, move-out cleaning and dentists. Meta when demand has to be created, or when you want to reach a region where few people search. Longer answer here.
Do you work with businesses outside Skien?+
Yes, most of our clients are elsewhere in the country, with branches from Agder to Trøndelag and in Oslo. Our office is in Skien. The work happens in your own Google Ads account, and we hold meetings over video.
What do you charge?+
You pay a share of the value we create, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.

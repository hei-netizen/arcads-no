Why Trondheim

It was not the click price that made Trondheim cheapest.

Cost per lead is the cost per click divided by the share of clicks that become an inquiry. Trondheim did not have the cheapest clicks. In Møre og Romsdal a click cost NOK 15, against NOK 19 in Trondheim. Even so, a lead in Møre og Romsdal cost NOK 81 more, because only 6.8% sent a form. In Trondheim, 13.4% did.

Turn it around and you see how much the rate matters. With Oslo’s conversion rate of 8.7%, Trondheim’s NOK 19 clicks would have given around NOK 218 per lead. With Trondheim’s rate, Oslo’s NOK 21 clicks would have given around NOK 157.

Why more people in Trondheim sent a form, we do not know for sure. The regions used the same national landing pages for almost the whole period, with the same offer. What we do know is that the conversion rate moved the price more than the click did.

A campaign of its own for Trondheim

In one campaign for the whole country, NOK 145 in Trondheim and NOK 237 in Oslo would have become an average, and nobody would have known Trondheim could take more budget. With its own campaign you see the city on its own and can move money to where leads are cheapest. How we set up multiple locations.

A local landing page, but tested

In August we built local pages for the branches, Trondheim included, and moved the ads there on 28 August. Since 9 September we have been testing them against the national pages in a Google Ads experiment. Until we have the answer, we only say this: the page is where the conversion rate is decided.

Ready before the season

For roofs and facades, March–May is high season and September–November is a second round. Smart bidding needs around 15–30 conversions a month to work well. That is why we want the campaign running and the tracking tested before high season, not in the middle of it.

Questions from Trondheim.

Why did Trondheim have the lowest cost per lead?+
Because the largest share of people who clicked sent a form. A click cost NOK 19, about the average, but the conversion rate was 13.4%, the highest of nine regions. Møre og Romsdal had cheaper clicks (NOK 15), but only 6.8% sent a form, and a lead cost NOK 226. Why Trondheim converted better, we do not know for sure.
Are Google AdWords and Google Ads the same thing?+
Yes. AdWords was renamed Google Ads in 2018. If you are looking for someone who knows Google AdWords in Trondheim, this is what we do: search ads, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube.
How big a budget does a Trondheim business need?+
We work with businesses that have at least NOK 30,000 a month for ads. In some industries the searches run out before the money does: “maler trondheim” (painter) gets 390 searches a month. Then we spread the budget across more searches and Meta, instead of bidding up the price of the same clicks.
When in the year should we start?+
Before the season, not during it. For roofs and facades, March–May is high season and September–November is a second round. Smart bidding needs around 15–30 conversions a month to work well, so the campaign should have been running for a while when the searches arrive.
Do you make commercials?+
We make video for ads: UGC, motion graphics and short 9:16 clips for Reels, Stories and Snapchat, in many variants for testing. We do not make TV commercials or film productions with a crew.
What do you charge?+
You pay a share of what we earn you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. See the pricing page.

How we run Google Ads in Oslo.

These are the moves we use in the Oslo accounts, with the numbers from them.

Geography

Oslo first, the ring after

Oslo municipality is the most expensive, but that is where the volume is. We run the surrounding municipalities in a separate ring campaign, so the cost per lead can be read on its own. For move-out cleaning, the ring is the municipalities within 30 minutes: Bærum, Asker, Lørenskog, Lillestrøm, Rælingen, Nittedal and Nordre Follo. For the moving company it is 60 minutes and includes the Drammen region. In the first month, a lead cost NOK 307 in the move-out cleaning ring and NOK 390 in the moving company ring.

Landing page

The page has to carry the click price

On search, a click cost NOK 52 for the moving company and NOK 57 for move-out cleaning. At NOK 52 per click, 10% conversion gives NOK 520 per lead, and 5% gives over NOK 1,000. That is why we track the conversion rate per landing page, and build a new page when the client’s own does not hold up. The numbers behind it.

Retargeting

The cheapest leads

A Performance Max campaign set up for retargeting delivered leads at NOK 116 for move-out cleaning and NOK 310 for the moving company, versus NOK 211 and 467 on search. The downside is that it also showed ads in mobile games and in kids’ videos on YouTube. That is why we check the placement report.

Brand

Your own name should not cost NOK 44

The search “rask flytting” (the company’s own name) cost around NOK 44 per click in the moving company’s regular search campaign and produced no bookings in June and July. Now it sits in a separate brand campaign with a cap of NOK 15 per click. That also makes the numbers for new customers more accurate.

Search terms

Searches that never become customers

In the move-out cleaning account, “billig flyttevask oslo” (cheap move-out cleaning oslo) cost NOK 298 in June and July without a single lead. Now it is a negative keyword. The search terms report shows what you are actually paying for, and we go through it regularly.

What Oslo businesses ask us.

What does Google Ads cost in Oslo?+
You set the budget yourself. What varies is the cost per click and per lead. In the accounts we run, a search click cost NOK 21 for facade and roof cleaning, NOK 52 for a moving company and NOK 57 for move-out cleaning. A lead cost NOK 202 (move-out cleaning), NOK 237 (facade and roof cleaning) and NOK 413 (moving company) in June–Sept 2026.
Are Google Ads and AdWords the same thing?+
Yes. Google AdWords was renamed Google Ads in 2018. Many people still use the old name, and it is the same system: search ads, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube and Display.
Should we advertise across all of Oslo and Akershus?+
Start with Oslo, and run the neighboring municipalities in a separate ring campaign so you see the cost per lead on its own. For the moving company, the ring had a lower cost per click than Oslo (NOK 42 vs. 52) and almost the same conversion rate. For move-out cleaning, the ring converted worse (16% vs. 27%). We let the ring run on its own for at least 3–4 weeks before we consider merging it with the main campaign.
You are based in Skien. Does that work for an Oslo business?+
Yes. The work happens in your own Google Ads account, and we hold meetings over video. Several of our clients are in Oslo, including the move-out cleaning company and the moving company in the table above.
What about Google Business Profile?+
Get it in order before you turn on ads. For local searches, Google shows the map with business profiles right below the ads, and the reviews there affect who gets the click.
What do you charge?+
You pay a share of the value we create, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.

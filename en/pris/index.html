Pricing

What does Arcads cost?

No fixed fee, no hourly rate and no percentage of the ad budget. We take 5–10% of the value the ads create for you, measured in your own account. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing.

Short answer: You pay a share of the value we create, 5–10%, not a fixed monthly fee. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough value that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That's why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta.

How the model works.

1

We agree on what value is

For an online store, it's the gross profit from the ads. For a lead-based business, it's inquiries × what an inquiry is worth to you. The number sits in your own account as conversion value, so it can be audited.

2

We set up tracking first

Google Ads, Meta Pixel and GA4 measure actual sales and leads before we spend a single krone. You see every sale in your own account, and you get a report every Monday.

3

The fee follows the numbers

You pay 5–10% of the value the ads created last month. We agree on the exact share before we start. If the ads haven't created anything extra, you pay us nothing.

Compared with the other models.

None of the models are dishonest. The difference is what the agency gets paid for.

The downside of our model: we have to be picky. Our share needs to come to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month, so we only take on clients where the ads can realistically create NOK 200,000–600,000 in value a month. Longer comparison here.

What people ask about pricing.

What does Arcads cost?+
You pay a share of the value we create, 5–10%, not a fixed monthly fee. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough value that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That's why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta.
What happens in a bad month?+
Then our share gets smaller, and if the ads haven't created anything extra, it's NOK 0. You will still have spent the ad budget, though. That's why we run a daily check on the Google accounts that alerts us if a campaign stops delivering, instead of finding out in the monthly report.
Is there a lock-in contract?+
No. No lock-in contract, and no notice period beyond the current month.
Is there a setup fee?+
No. Tracking, structure, landing pages and the first ads are part of the job, and we get paid through our share of what the ads create.
Why do you turn some clients down?+
Because our share needs to come to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. At 5–10%, that means the ads need to be able to create NOK 200,000–600,000 in value every month. If the margin is too low, the search volume too small or the budget under NOK 30,000, it doesn't add up, and we'll tell you so before you've paid anything.
How do I know what you've created?+
We set up tracking (Google Ads, Meta Pixel, GA4) before we spend a single krone, and you see every sale and every lead in your own account. The report you get every Monday shows what you spent and what it brought in. That's the basis for the invoice.
What is “value” for a lead-based business?+
Number of inquiries × what you've agreed an inquiry is worth (value per customer × share that become customers). We set the number together with you at the start, and it shows up in your own Google Ads account as conversion value.

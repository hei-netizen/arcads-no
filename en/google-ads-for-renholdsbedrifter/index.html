How we run Google Ads for cleaning.

Clicks are cheap in cleaning, NOK 17–26 on average in our accounts. But many searches come from people who want to do the job themselves or are looking for a job.

Search terms

You don’t pay for “vaske taket selv” (clean the roof yourself)

In September we went through 917 search terms from the last three months in the facade and roof cleaning account and added 25 new negative keywords: DIY, competitors, wrong service and pure price questions. They had cost around NOK 1,700 and produced one lead.

Geography

One campaign per region

The cost per lead ranged from NOK 145 in Trondheim to NOK 341 in Østfold for the same service. You only see that when the regions have their own campaigns. More on multiple locations.

Landing page

Photos, price and a short form

Cleaning sells on photos. The landing page should show before/after, say something about price and have a short form. Whether separate pages per city give cheaper leads than one good national page is something we are testing for Utvendig Renhold right now.

Season

Spring and fall sell differently

March–May is peak season for facades and roofs. September–November it sells as “get your house ready for winter.” Ad copy and budget have to follow the season.

Measurement

Count what is actually an inquiry

In the facade and roof cleaning account, only submitted forms count. In the move-out cleaning account, clicks on the phone number and email also count, and they were 27% of conversions. A click on a number is not a call, so we read those numbers with caution.

Brand

Branded searches kept separate

Someone who searches for your company name would have found you anyway. In the facade and roof cleaning account, a lead from branded searches cost NOK 47. Mix those in, and the new-customer ads look cheaper than they are: NOK 163 with, about NOK 188 without.

What cleaning companies ask us.

What does a lead cost for a cleaning company on Google?+
In the accounts we run: NOK 163 per lead for facade and roof cleaning across 9 regions including branded searches (about NOK 188 without), and NOK 202 for move-out cleaning in Oslo, June–Sept 2026. The cheapest region was NOK 145, the most expensive NOK 341.
Does Google Ads work for move-out cleaning and office cleaning?+
For move-out cleaning, yes. In the account we run in Oslo, 12.9% of those who clicked got in touch, and a lead cost NOK 202. People have to be out of their home by a set date, so they book quickly. We have no numbers of our own for office cleaning, so we cannot say what a lead costs there.
Should we have one campaign for all of Norway?+
Not if you have multiple branches. For the same service, the cost per lead ranged from NOK 145 to 341 between regions. With one campaign per region you can see that and allocate the budget accordingly. How we set up multiple locations.
How do we avoid inquiries from people who want to do it themselves?+
With negative keywords like “selv” (yourself), “hvordan” (how), “tips,” “jobb” (job), “stilling” (position) and “gratis” (free), and ad copy that mentions fixed prices and on-site inspections. That filters out many people before they click. The search terms report shows what slips through.
What about seasonality?+
Facades and roofs peak in March–May, with another round in September–November. Ad copy and budget should follow that, for example with “get your house ready for winter” in the fall.
What do you charge?+
You pay a share of the value we create, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.

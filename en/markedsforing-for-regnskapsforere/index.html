Marketing for accountants

Marketing for accountants: be there when a business switches.

Most small businesses don’t switch accountants until something happens. They start up, outgrow their sole proprietorship, or are unhappy after the tax return. That’s when they search. Here is how we would set up Google Ads to meet them right then.

Short answer: Accountants should advertise on Google to businesses that are starting up or want to switch: “regnskapsfører [by]” (accountant [city]), “regnskapsfører enkeltpersonforetak” (accountant for sole proprietorship), “bytte regnskapsfører” (switch accountant). The landing page should show a “from” price per month, which systems you work in (Fiken, Tripletex, PowerOffice) and a form that asks about company type and number of vouchers. Google is the main channel. LinkedIn and Facebook can come later.

Four ad groups, four reasons to search.

A business that wants to switch and one that has just been registered need different answers. We would split the account by why people search.

Ad group

Switching

“bytte regnskapsfører” (switch accountant), “ny regnskapsfører” (new accountant), “regnskapsfører [by]” (accountant [city]). The page answers what makes switching hard: how the handover works, and who contacts the old accountant.

Ad group

Start-ups and sole proprietorships

“regnskapsfører enkeltpersonforetak” (accountant for sole proprietorship), “starte AS regnskap” (start limited company accounting). Here the “from” price matters most, along with an explanation of what a new company has to file in its first year.

Ad group

System

“regnskapsfører Fiken” (accountant Fiken), “regnskapsfører Tripletex” (accountant Tripletex). Anyone who already uses a system wants someone who knows it. Searches for just “Fiken” are people looking for the software, and are kept out.

Ad group

Industry

“regnskapsfører for håndverkere” (accountant for tradespeople), “regnskap restaurant” (restaurant accounting). If there are industries you know well, they get their own ads and a page that shows it.

A “from” price per month on the page

A business comparing accountants wants to know roughly what it costs before getting in touch. A “from” price per month, for example for a sole proprietorship with few vouchers, is enough. Add what makes the price go up: number of vouchers, employee payroll, VAT registration. Then customers know where they’ll land, and you don’t have to work out a quote for everyone who was just curious.

Also say which systems you work in. Fiken, Tripletex and PowerOffice are what many small businesses use, and many don’t want to change systems when they change accountants.

The form that qualifies

We would ask about company type, roughly how many vouchers a month, which system they use today and when they want to switch. Four fields. Then you can see who should be called first, and who would be a better fit elsewhere.

Negative keywords

“Regnskap” (accounting) on its own is largely students, courses and people looking for software. We would keep out: kurs (course), jobb (job), lønn (salary), utdanning (education), gratis (free), mal (template), regnskapsprogram (accounting software), and the names of the accounting programs when they appear on their own.

When they search

The tax return deadline for self-employed businesses is May 31, and the turn of the year is a natural time to switch. We would check the search volume for your city in Google’s Keyword Planner before setting the budget around it.

What accountants ask us.

What can a lead cost for an accounting firm?+
We don’t have numbers from the accounting industry. The closest is B2B leasing for Generalfinans, where a lead costs NOK 406 with branded searches and NOK 517 without. That’s one account in a different industry. For you, what a client is worth matters more: if the client pays NOK 30,000 a year and stays for three years, a lead can cost a lot.
Google or LinkedIn for accountants?+
Google for those searching now, because they’re switching or starting up. LinkedIn has high costs per click and fits better when you want bigger clients. Facebook can reach owners of new companies in your area.
Should we advertise our price?+
Yes. A “from” price like “accounting for sole proprietorships from NOK 990/month” (example) means those only looking for the cheapest option sort themselves out. Without a price, you get the question in every conversation anyway.
How do we reach newly started companies?+
With keywords like “regnskapsfører enkeltpersonforetak” (accountant for sole proprietorship) and “starte AS regnskap” (start limited company accounting), and a page that answers what new companies wonder about: what do I have to file, when, and what does it cost.
Have you worked with accounting firms before?+
No. We have worked with B2B leasing for Generalfinans and with businesses that live on inquiries. The setup on this page is how we would do it for you.

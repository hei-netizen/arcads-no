The challenge

Nine locations, nine different markets

A national campaign gives you no control over where the leads land — Bergen can drown in requests while Østfold gets nothing.

The franchise needed local control: its own budget, its own ads and its own numbers for every single location.

Franchise with 9 areas Different local markets Seasonal variation
What we did

A separate campaign per location — on both Meta and Google

Each location has its own campaigns with geo-targeted ads and its own budget, on Meta and Google at the same time. If something works in Bergen, we roll it out to the rest.

Every week the chain gets a report per location — leads, cost and CPL for each area, not just the total.

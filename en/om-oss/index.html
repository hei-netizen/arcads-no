About us

There are two of us. That's the whole agency.

Arcads is Victor Skretteberg and Jona Helgen. We're the ones you talk to, and we're the ones doing the work in your account.

Arcads is a Norwegian performance marketing agency (Arcads ANS, founded 2025, office in Skien). We run Google Ads and Meta Ads and make the ads ourselves, for service businesses and online stores all over Norway.

What we do every week.

We make new ads for our clients every week, more than 10 a week in total and more than 5,000 since we started. Statics, UGC video and motion graphics, for Meta and Google.

In Google Ads we go through search terms, bids and budgets. We change one thing at a time and write down in advance when we'll read the result. Every Monday our clients get a report with the numbers from the week.

We use AI tools a lot, both for analysis and for ad production. That's how two people can get all this done.

Our clients.

Utvendig Renhold (facade and roof cleaning in 9 regions), Veldigrent and Raskflytting (move-out cleaning and moving in Oslo, same owner), Badstugutta (saunas), Mokki (sunglasses for kids, selling in Norway, Germany and Denmark), QualiPet (dog treats), Generalfinans (leasing for businesses), Frøyd (aesthetic clinic), Redz (tattoo supplies), Frifor.app and a dental clinic in England that we run Meta for. Leid.no (trailer rental) is on pause for now.

How we charge.

You pay a share of the value we create, not a fixed monthly fee. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough value that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That's why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.

What I do

I set up and run the Google Ads accounts: conversion tracking, campaign structure, search terms, bids and budgets. For online stores, that's mostly Shopping and Performance Max. For service businesses, it's search campaigns, often one per region.

I change one thing at a time and write down in advance what I expect and when I'll read the result. Otherwise it's impossible to know what worked.

Accounts I work in

  • Utvendig Renhold: 549 leads in 90 days from 9 regions, NOK 163 per lead including branded searches (June–Sept 2026).
  • Veldigrent and Raskflytting in Oslo: NOK 202 per lead for move-out cleaning and NOK 413 per booking request for the moving company.
  • Mokki: Google Ads in Norway, Germany and Denmark. Mokki is at +127% profit year over year, by their own numbers.

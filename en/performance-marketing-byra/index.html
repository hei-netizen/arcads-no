What is performance marketing?

In short

Performance marketing means every krone spent on ads should be traceable to something that happened: a purchase, a form, a phone call. That requires tracking that counts correctly, campaigns split so the numbers mean something, and enough new ads to be able to test. Brand building where the effect can't be measured directly is a different discipline, and not what we do.

What people ask about performance marketing.

What is a performance marketing agency?+
An agency that runs paid advertising and measures it on what happens after the click: purchases, submitted forms, bookings. In practice, Google Ads, Meta and Snapchat, with tracking that shows what every krone brought in. With us, the fee is also tied to the results.
What does a performance marketing agency cost?+
With us, we take a share of the value we create for you, 5–10%, not a fixed monthly price. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For this to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough value that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That's why we work with established businesses with at least NOK 30,000 in monthly ad budget. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. The full calculation on the pricing page.
What's the difference between a performance-based and a fixed fee?+
With a fixed fee, the agency gets paid no matter how the month went. With a performance-based fee, the agency only gets paid when you earn more. The catch is that the agency has to be selective: we can only take on clients where the ads can realistically create enough value for our share to reach NOK 20,000–30,000 a month.
Which channels do you work with?+
Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max), Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Snapchat. Plus landing pages, conversion tracking and ad production.
Is performance marketing a fit for small businesses?+
It depends on the numbers. The margin and the value of a customer have to be high enough for paid traffic to pay off. With our model, the ads need to be able to create NOK 200,000–600,000 in value a month, and that usually requires at least NOK 30,000 in ad budget. If you're below that, we'll say so in the proposal we send you.
How do you measure results?+
Tracking is set up before we spend a single krone: Google Ads, Meta Pixel and GA4, measured against purchases in the online store or forms in your inbox. You get a report every Monday with what you spent, what it brought in, and what we're changing this week.

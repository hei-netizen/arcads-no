What does Google Ads cost in Norway?

The cost of Google Ads has two parts: what you pay Google for the clicks, and what running the account costs, in your own time or at an agency. Here are both, with click prices and cost per lead from the accounts we manage.

By Victor Skretteberg, Arcads · Updated September 28, 2026

Short answer: Google Ads has no fixed price. You set the budget yourself and pay per click. In eight accounts we manage, a search click cost NOK 11–79, a Shopping click NOK 4–17 and a Performance Max click NOK 6–28 (June–September 2026, branded searches excluded). On top of that comes the cost of managing the account.

Google Ads cost per click: numbers from our accounts

Google Ads is an auction. You pay when someone clicks, and the price is set by how many advertisers bid on the same search and how well your ad and landing page match it. For services, a search click cost NOK 14–79; for the online stores, NOK 11–16. Services in Oslo and B2B were at the top. Branded searches, meaning people searching for your company name, cost NOK 4–22 per click, and under NOK 10 in five of the eight accounts.

Keep Search and Performance Max (PMax) apart when you read numbers like these. PMax also buys placements on YouTube, Display and Gmail, where clicks are cheap because people haven’t searched for you. A PMax click at NOK 6 is therefore not better than a search click at NOK 55. What counts is what a lead or a sale costs.

What it costs per lead

Excluding branded searches, a lead in the same accounts cost about NOK 188 for facade and roof cleaning, NOK 202 for move-out cleaning, NOK 412 for a moving booking, NOK 517 for business leasing and about NOK 1,090 for saunas. The numbers with caveats, and the difference between Search and PMax, are in What does a lead cost in Norway?.

How big a budget do you need?

Google has no minimum spend. But for smart bidding (target CPA or target ROAS) to work, a campaign needs around 15–30 conversions over 30 days. With fewer, the algorithm has too little to go on, and prices jump around.

Monthly budget per campaign ≈ expected cost per lead × 15–30.
If you’re at NOK 200 per lead, the campaign needs NOK 3,000–6,000 a month. At NOK 500, it’s NOK 7,500–15,000. If you split the account into many campaigns, each of them needs enough data.

If you sell online, you work it the other way: breakeven ROAS is 1 divided by your gross margin. With a 40% margin, you need NOK 2.50 back for every krone spent on ads. If your online store sends revenue including VAT to Google, you have to multiply by 1.25, and breakeven becomes 3.1x. More on that in What is a good ROAS?.

Cost 2: management

An account needs attention every week: the search terms have to be reviewed, budget moved between campaigns, and tracking has to count real sales and inquiries. The most expensive mistake we have found in accounts we’ve taken over is tracking that counts wrong. When we started with Leid.no, tracking counted far more purchases than actually happened, so Google was optimizing toward numbers that didn’t exist. You have three ways to get the job done:

  • Do it yourself. No fee, but you pay in time, and mistakes in tracking and keywords can quickly cost more than a fee. Here’s how to get started.
  • An agency with a fixed monthly fee or a percentage of the ad budget. The most common model. You know what it costs, but the fee is the same whether the ads deliver or not, and with a percentage of budget, the agency earns more when you spend more.
  • Performance-based. This is how we charge: a share of the value we create for you, typically 5–10%. If you don’t earn more, you pay us nothing. Pros and cons of both models.

Whichever model you choose, you pay the ad budget directly to Google.

Questions and answers.

How much does a click cost in Google Ads in Norway?+
In eight accounts we manage, a search click cost NOK 11–79, a Shopping click NOK 4–17 and a Performance Max click NOK 6–28 in the period June–September 2026, excluding branded searches. Services in Oslo and B2B were at the top, online stores at the bottom.
How big a budget do I need for Google Ads?+
Google has no minimum spend. For smart bidding to work, a campaign should get around 15–30 conversions a month. Multiply your expected cost per lead by 15–30, and you have a realistic monthly budget: NOK 3,000–6,000 at NOK 200 per lead, NOK 7,500–15,000 at NOK 500.
What does it cost to have an agency manage Google Ads?+
Most agencies charge a fixed monthly fee or a percentage of the ad budget. We take a share of the value we create for you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you don’t earn more, you pay us nothing. For this to work for both sides, the ads need to create enough value for our share to be around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That’s why we work with established businesses with at least NOK 30,000 in monthly ad budget. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta.
Why are clicks cheaper in Performance Max than in Search?+
Performance Max also shows ads on YouTube, Display and Gmail, where clicks are cheap because people haven’t searched for what you sell. So compare cost per lead or sale between campaign types, not cost per click.
Can I manage Google Ads myself?+
Yes. It requires conversion tracking that counts real sales or inquiries, going through the search terms every week and adding negative keywords, and evaluating Google’s recommendations before you accept them. Here’s how to get started with Google advertising.

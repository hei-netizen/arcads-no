What does Google Ads cost in Norway?

The cost of Google Ads has two parts. One is what you pay Google for the clicks, the other is running the account, either in your own time or at an agency. Here are both, with fresh click prices from eight accounts we manage and Keyword Planner numbers for 26 common searches.

By Victor Skretteberg, Arcads · Published August 18, 2026 · Updated November 28, 20269 min read

Short answer: Google Ads has no fixed price. You decide the budget and pay per click, and the price is set in an auction every time someone searches. In eight accounts we manage, a search click cost NOK 7–114, a Shopping click NOK 4–11 and a Performance Max click NOK 5–16 (30 August–27 November 2026, searches for the client's own name excluded). Industry and city matter most. According to Keyword Planner, a top spot on "rørlegger oslo" (plumber Oslo) costs NOK 89–306 per click, while "frisør" (hairdresser) costs NOK 4–19.

Why do some advertisers pay more per click than others?

Every time someone searches, Google calculates an Ad Rank for each advertiser. It is based on six things (Google on Ad Rank):

  • your bid
  • the quality of your ad and landing page
  • the minimum thresholds for showing at all
  • how competitive the auction is for that exact search
  • the context of the search, such as location and device
  • the expected impact of your ad assets, for example sitelinks and a phone number

You don't pay your bid. You pay what it takes to beat the advertiser just below you and clear the thresholds, and that is often much less than your bid (Google on actual CPC). Two businesses bidding on the same search can therefore pay very different amounts per click.

The 1–10 Quality Score you see in the account is based on expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. Google itself says the score is not an input in the auction. It is a diagnostic tool, but it points to the same things that decide the price (Google on Quality Score).

Same business, nine regions, different prices

Utvendig Renhold has one search campaign per region, with the same setup and a local landing page for each. Even so, a search click cost from NOK 19 in Møre og Romsdal to NOK 29 in Rogaland (30 August–27 November 2026). Oslo was in the middle at NOK 23. The setup is the same, so most of the difference comes from the competition in each region. You can only see that in your own account.

Searches for your own name should be cheap

In the moving company's account, searches for the company's own name, "rask flytting", cost around NOK 44 per click as long as they sat in the regular search campaign. We moved them into a separate brand campaign with a NOK 15 cap. From 30 August to 27 November they cost NOK 13 per click.

What it costs per lead and per sale

The click price says little on its own, because what you end up paying for is leads and sales. In the five lead accounts, a lead from search cost NOK 537 on average from 30 August to 27 November 2026, excluding searches for the client's own name. From Performance Max it cost NOK 697, even though the click there was four times cheaper.

In the three online stores, a sale cost NOK 266 from search and NOK 249 from Shopping, even though the Shopping click cost less than half. People who click a product ad with a picture and a price buy less often per click, but the click is cheaper.

A table with cost per click, conversion rate and cost per customer for each campaign type is in our article on online advertising. What a lead costs in different industries is covered in What does a lead cost in Norway?

How big a budget do you need?

Google has no minimum spend. You set an average daily budget per campaign. Google can spend up to twice that on a single day, but never more than 30.4 times the daily budget in a month (Google on daily budgets). If you want to spend NOK 10,000 a month, you set the daily budget to around NOK 330.

How much you should spend depends on what a lead or a sale costs. For smart bidding (target CPA or target ROAS) to work, a campaign needs around 15–30 conversions over 30 days. With fewer, the algorithm has too little to go on, and prices jump around.

Monthly budget per campaign ≈ expected cost per lead × 15–30.
At NOK 537 per lead, which was the average from search in our lead accounts, that is NOK 8,000–16,000 a month for one search campaign. At NOK 200, NOK 3,000–6,000 is enough. If you split the account into many campaigns, each of them needs enough data.

For comparison, the eight accounts we manage spent from NOK 2,900 to NOK 64,000 a month on Google Ads in September and October 2026. The median was NOK 23,000.

If you sell online, you work it the other way. Breakeven ROAS is 1 divided by your gross margin. With a 40% margin, you need NOK 2.5 back per NOK spent on ads. If the store sends revenue including VAT to Google, multiply by 1.25, and breakeven becomes 3.1x. More on that in What is a good ROAS?

Click prices change through the year

The search click got more expensive in four of the five lead accounts from June–August to September–November 2026, by anything from 14% to 120%. In the moving company's account it got 25% cheaper. The average for all five went from NOK 25 to NOK 33 per click. We can't separate what is down to season and competitors from what is down to changes we made in the accounts ourselves. A budget you set in June won't necessarily hold in October. Follow the click price month by month in your own account.

Cost 2: management

An account needs attention every week. The search terms have to be reviewed, budget moved between campaigns, and the tracking has to count real sales and leads. The most expensive mistake we have found in accounts we've taken over is tracking that counts wrong. When we started with Leid.no, the tracking counted far more purchases than actually happened, so Google optimized toward numbers that didn't exist. You have three ways to get the job done.

  • Do it yourself. You pay no fee, but you pay in time, and mistakes in tracking and keywords quickly cost more than a fee. How to get started.
  • An agency on a fixed monthly fee or a percentage of ad spend. This is the most common model (see what a Google Ads agency charges). You know what it costs, but the fee is the same whether the ads deliver or not. With a percentage of spend, the agency earns more when you spend more.
  • Results-based. This is how we charge. We get 5–10% of the value we create for you, and if you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both sides, our share needs to be able to reach around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with businesses that have at least NOK 30,000 a month in ad budget. Whatever the model, you pay the ad budget directly to Google. If you want the pros and cons side by side, we've written about results-based vs fixed-fee agencies.

Questions and answers.

How much does a click cost in Google Ads in Norway?+
It depends on industry, city and campaign type. In eight accounts we manage, a search click cost NOK 7–114, a Shopping click NOK 4–11 and a Performance Max click NOK 5–16 (30 August–27 November 2026, searches for the client's own name excluded). According to Keyword Planner, a top spot costs NOK 4–19 on "frisør" (hairdresser) and NOK 89–306 on "rørlegger oslo" (plumber Oslo).
What does Google Ads cost per month?+
You decide, because Google has no minimum spend. Google never spends more than 30.4 times the daily budget in a month. A realistic budget for one search campaign is the expected cost per lead multiplied by 15–30. At NOK 537 per lead, which was the average from search in our lead accounts, that is NOK 8,000–16,000. The eight accounts we manage spent NOK 2,900–64,000 a month in September and October 2026, with a median of NOK 23,000.
Can you trust Keyword Planner?+
As a guide, yes. The numbers show what advertisers have paid for a top spot over the last 30 days. In five of eight accounts we manage, the actual cost per click was inside the range for the main search, while three paid more than the high estimate. Plan with the high number when you set a budget.
Why does my competitor pay less per click than me?+
The price is set in an auction where Google also looks at expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. You pay what it takes to beat the advertiser just below you, and that is often much less than your bid. Better ads and a landing page that answers the search can therefore get you the same spot for less.
Why are Performance Max clicks cheaper than search clicks?+
Performance Max also shows ads on YouTube, Display and Gmail, where clicks are cheap because people haven't searched for what you sell. In five lead accounts we manage, a PMax click cost NOK 8 and a search click NOK 33, but a lead cost NOK 697 from PMax and NOK 537 from search (Aug–Nov 2026). So compare cost per lead or sale, not cost per click.
What does it cost to have an agency run Google Ads?+
Most agencies charge a fixed monthly fee or a percentage of ad spend. We take 5–10% of the value we create for you, not a fixed monthly price. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both sides, our share needs to be able to reach around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with businesses that have at least NOK 30,000 a month in ad budget. You pay the ad budget directly to Google.
Can I run Google Ads myself?+
Yes. It requires conversion tracking that counts real sales or leads, a weekly review of search terms with negative keywords added, and that you assess Google's recommendations before accepting them. How to get started with Google advertising.

When you should choose a production company instead

  • You are going on TV, in cinemas or on big screens.
  • You need a brand film with actors, crew and a location that will be used for years.
  • The film is for your website or sales meetings, not for testing in ads.
  • You want to buy one film and run the ads yourself or with another agency.

We do not make TV commercials and we do not have a crew. What we do is make videos that can be measured every week, and replace them when the numbers say so.

Anatomy

How a video ad that sells is built.

The example is the format that has had the best response of what we have tested in motion: problem and solution, mirrored, around 45 seconds.

Hook
5 problems
Turn
5 solutions
End
0 s20 s41 s47 s
  1. The first second: something concrete. A real product, a real screenshot, a situation people recognise, or a number. Nice typography on an empty background stops no one.
  2. The problem: shown as small scenes, not icon lists. A clock going from 21:47 to 23:12 says more than the word “time-consuming”.
  3. The solution: in the same order as the problems, so viewers only need to recognise themselves. The music shifts when the solution arrives.
  4. The end frame: what you offer and where to go next.

A regular ad has around 20 seconds of speech. Something new should happen at least every 1.5 to 3 seconds, and text should stay long enough to be read, at least 2 seconds.

The sound is mixed so the voice always sits on top: the music stays evenly low under the whole voice, and the full video is normalised to -15 LUFS.

Testing

Why many variations beat one expensive film.

We do not know which video works until it has run. Nobody does. That is why we spend the money on several attempts, not on one.

1

The angle first

We start with why people buy. For Badstugutta, the angles that bring in leads have been a sauna delivered fully assembled, a fixed price and delivery in around four weeks. The format is chosen after that.

2

One shoot, many ads

The same footage is cut with different hooks and lengths, in 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1. New ads go into ad sets that already have data, so the test does not reset the learning phase.

3

Measured on sales and leads

A video is judged on cost per sale or lead, not on views. What delivers gets more budget and new variations. The rest we switch off. We make 10+ new ads a week per client.

With one expensive film you get one attempt. If the hook misses, you have paid for a film few people watch to the end. With many simple variations, the account finds the one that works, and we make more of it.

Commercial pricing

What does a commercial cost?

With us: we do not sell videos one by one. The videos are part of running your ads. You pay a share of what we earn you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That is why we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. How it is calculated.

What people ask about commercials.

Do you make TV commercials?+
No. We make commercials for social media and ads: short videos in 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 that run on Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat. If you are going on TV or in cinemas, or need a brand film with a crew and actors, you should choose a production company.
What does a commercial cost with you?+
We do not sell videos one by one. The videos are part of running your ads, and you pay a share of what we earn you, not a fixed monthly fee. If you do not earn more, you pay us nothing. For it to work for both of us, our share needs to come to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month, so we work with established businesses with a monthly ad budget of at least NOK 30,000. See the pricing page.
Can we buy video production only, without ad management?+
Usually not. We make video because the ad account is where we see what works, and our fee is a share of what the ads create. If you have a specific need, email victor@arcads.no and we will tell you honestly whether it is a fit.
How long should a video ad for social media be?+
A regular ad from us has around 20 seconds of speech. The problem and solution format is longer, around 45 seconds, because it shows five problems and five solutions. Whatever the length, something concrete should happen in the first second.
What is the difference between motion graphics and motion design?+
The terms are mostly used for the same thing: text, graphics and images in motion. For us, motion graphics means an animated ad with a Norwegian voiceover and music, where every animation is locked to the word in the voice. How we make them.
Do we need our own video footage?+
No, but it helps. Footage from the job, product video and customer clips are often better than anything a creator can film, because they are real. If you have nothing, we start with creators working from a script, motion graphics and statics.

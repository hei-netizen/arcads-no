The maths

How much can you afford to pay for a purchase?

Before we set a ROAS target, we work out how much a purchase can cost before the ads lose money.

Breakeven ROAS = 1 / gross margin. With a 40% margin, breakeven is 2.5x. Gross margin is what you have left after cost of goods, shipping and fees. The same rule can be written in kroner per order: maximum cost per purchase = average order × gross margin.

POAS: when margins vary

POAS (profit on ad spend) is gross profit divided by ad spend. Above 1 means the ads make money. Mokki had a blended ROAS of 6.67x and a POAS of 2.61x in March 2026. If your products have very different margins, we steer by POAS instead, or split products into campaigns by margin. Remember VAT too: if the account reports order value including 25% VAT, multiply breakeven by 1.25. What is a good ROAS?

The tracking trap

When the ad account shows more purchases than the till.

When we started with Leid.no, the tracking counted far more purchases than actually happened. Every budget decision had been made on numbers that looked better than reality.

1

The same purchase counted twice

We have also seen a Google account where the same purchase was counted twice. ROAS then looked twice as good as it was. We place a test order and compare the number of purchases in Google Ads with the online store.

2

VAT in the value

If order value is sent including VAT, an order of NOK 1,250 shows as NOK 1,250 in the account even though your revenue is NOK 1,000. We send the value excluding VAT, or adjust the target.

3

Several channels take the credit

If the customer has been through both Google and Meta, both count the sale. That is why we also track total revenue divided by total ad spend, not just ROAS per channel.

Sales periods

The sales campaign that delivered 10.3x ROAS.

QualiPet was running a sales campaign. A sale only gets seen if three things are in place before it starts:

  1. The prices in the feed. The feed extension in the online store did not send the sale prices, so the discounts would never have shown in the Shopping ads. We added them with a supplemental feed in Merchant Center.
  2. A dedicated landing page for the campaign, with the products and discounts in one place.
  3. A dedicated campaign on Meta with ads for the sale.

Result on Meta, September 2026: 10.3x ROAS.

Which channel should an online store start with?+
Usually Google Shopping or Performance Max, because they show your products to people already searching for them. Branded searches go in a separate campaign. We add Meta and Snapchat to create demand. But first, the tracking has to count every purchase once.
What is a good ROAS for an online store?+
One that sits well above breakeven. Breakeven ROAS is 1 divided by your gross margin: with a 40% margin it is 2.5x, with a 20% margin 5x. Leid.no is at 4.73x across channels, and Mokki had 6.67x blended in March 2026. More on ROAS.
What is POAS?+
Profit on ad spend: gross profit divided by ad spend. Above 1 means the ads make money. It is a better steering metric than ROAS when margins vary a lot between products. Mokki had a POAS of 2.61x in March 2026.
Why do the purchases in Google Ads not match my online store?+
Common reasons are that the same purchase is counted twice, that the value is sent including VAT, or that both Google and Meta take credit for the same sale. When we started with Leid.no, the tracking counted far more purchases than actually happened. We fix tracking before we scale anything.
Do you do SEO or TikTok for online stores?+
No. We run Google Ads (search, Shopping, Performance Max and YouTube), Meta and Snapchat, and make the ads and landing pages ourselves. We do not do SEO, organic social media management or TikTok ads.

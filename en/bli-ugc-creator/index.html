For creators

Become a UGC creator for Arcads.

We make ads for Norwegian brands, and we need people who can film on their phone from our scripts. You don't need any followers — just a face the camera likes, Norwegian as your spoken language, and the ability to deliver on deadline. Paid per job, agreed before you film. The application is four lines in an email.

Apply by emailvictor@arcads.no · four lines, no attachments

How a job works.

1

You get a script and brief

We write the hooks and scripts — you don't have to come up with anything yourself. You're told exactly what to say, how to film it, and when to deliver it.

2

You film at home

Phone, daylight, good sound. You send us the raw clips — we handle editing, captions and formats. UGC shouldn't look studio-produced; that's the whole point.

3

Price agreed before you film

Put your price per video in your application. If you can make one free test video in exchange for the product, you are prioritised. Deliver good work, and paid jobs follow.

The content is used in our clients' ads — see how we work with UGC and what UGC actually is if you're new to this.

What we look for.

Here's the list:

Most important

Natural on camera

You should sound like a friend recommending something — not like an ad voice. If you freeze up on camera, the viewer notices within a second.

Language

Norwegian, with or without a dialect

The ads run in Norway. A dialect is a plus, not a minus — it makes you more real.

Reliability

Delivers on deadline

Ads go live on a schedule. Creators who deliver what we agreed on, when we agreed on it, get jobs again and again.

Not required

Followers, equipment, experience

Zero requirements for any of them. We'll teach you the technical side, and your phone is the equipment.

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need followers to become a UGC creator?+
No. UGC is about the content, not your reach — the videos are used in ads for the brands we work for, not on your channel. We care about how you are on camera, not how many people follow you.
Do I need professional equipment?+
No — a recent phone with good sound and daylight is all you need. UGC shouldn't look studio-produced; that's the whole point. We handle editing, captions and formats.
How much does a UGC creator earn?+
It depends on the scope (number of videos, script length, usage rights), and the price is agreed before you film. Put your price per video in your application. If you can make one free test video in exchange for the product, we prioritise you when we have a job.
How do I apply?+
Send a short email to victor@arcads.no with the subject “UGC-creator” and four things: your name and where you live, one link to your portfolio, Instagram or TikTok, your price per video, and whether you can make one free test video in exchange for the product. Don't send videos or attachments. We get in touch when we have a job that fits, and creators who can do a free test video are prioritised.

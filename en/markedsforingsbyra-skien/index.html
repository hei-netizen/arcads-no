Google Ads agency in Telemark

Google Ads and Meta for businesses in Grenland and Telemark.

Skien, Porsgrunn, Bamble, Notodden, Bø, Kragerø, Rjukan. We have an office in Skien and are happy to hold the first meeting there.

Telemark has lower search volume than Oslo. That means fewer leads per month, and it takes longer before Google has enough data for smart bidding, which needs around 15–30 conversions a month. For a business in Grenland, one well-built campaign is therefore often better than many small ones.

For businesses in Telemark, we do the same as for our clients elsewhere in the country: Google Ads measured on inquiries, Meta ads we make ourselves, and a report every Monday. The difference is that the first meeting can be at the office on Jonas Lies gate. We're a fit for established businesses with at least NOK 30,000 in monthly ad budget, wherever they're located.

Questions and answers.

Is there a marketing agency in Skien?+
Yes. Arcads ANS is based at Jonas Lies gate 1 in central Skien. We run Google Ads and Meta Ads and make the ads ourselves.
Do you only work with businesses in Skien and Telemark?+
No. The office is in Skien, but most of our clients are elsewhere in Norway. We also run ads for a dental clinic in England and for an online store that sells in Germany and Denmark. Businesses in Grenland are welcome to drop by for a coffee. With everyone else, we meet on video.
What does it cost?+
We take a share of the value we create for you, not a fixed monthly price. If you don't earn more, you pay us nothing. For this to work for both of us, the ads need to create enough value that our share comes to around NOK 20,000–30,000 a month. That's why we work with established businesses with at least NOK 30,000 in monthly ad budget. You pay the ad budget directly to Google and Meta. See the pricing page.
